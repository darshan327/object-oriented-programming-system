1) explain oops concepts with real world example like car

    Encapsulation — "Protect the data"

    In a car, you don't directly access the engine's internal components.

      You simply use:
      
      Accelerator
      Brake
      Steering


    Abstraction — "Hide complexity" 

      When you drive a car:

       Press brake
            ↓
       Car stops



     Inheritance — "Reuse existing properties"   

                  Vehicle
                │
       ┌────────┴────────┐
       ↓                 ↓
      Car              Bike

         Both are vehicles.
          
          A vehicle can have:
          
          start()
          stop()
       
     Polymorphism — "Same interface, different behavior"
       
      Vehicle
       │
       ├── Car → moves using engine
       │
       ├── Bike → moves using engine
       │
       └── ElectricCar → moves using electric motor
